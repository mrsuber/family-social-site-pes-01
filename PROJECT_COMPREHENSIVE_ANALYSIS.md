## PART 3: SUBERCRAFTEX PLATFORM

### Overview
A **crowdfunded manufacturing ecosystem** where investors fund inventory and equipment, then automatically receive profit-sharing when products sell or equipment is used.

### Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript 5, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui
- **Backend:** Next.js API Routes, Node.js, Express
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM (25+ models)
- **Authentication:** JWT tokens, bcrypt hashing
- **Payments:** Stripe API
- **Deployment:** VPS (148.230.118.19), PM2, Nginx, Ubuntu

### The Investor Model (Revolutionary)

**How It Works:**
1. Investor registers and completes KYC
2. Deposits funds (cash/bank transfer/mobile money)
3. Admin allocates capital to products or equipment
4. When products sell or equipment is used:
   - Company receives 50% of profit
   - Investor receives 50% of profit (into Profit Balance)
   - Capital returns to Cash Balance (separate from profit)
5. Investor can withdraw, reallocate, or claim products

**Example - Product Investment:**
```
Investor: 1,000,000 XAF
Allocated: 100 t-shirts @ 10K each

When 1 t-shirt sells for 15K:
  Profit: 5,000 XAF
  Company: 2,500 (50%)
  Investor: 2,500 (50%) → Profit Balance
  Capital: 10,000 → Cash Balance

After all 100 sell:
  Investor Total: 1,250,000 XAF
    - Cash: 1,000,000 (capital back)
    - Profit: 250,000 (earnings, 25% ROI)
```

**Why Investors Win:**
- No equity dilution (no company ownership)
- Capital protection (separate from profit)
- 50% profit share (much better than bank interest)
- Transparency and flexibility
- Multiple independent bets

**Why Company Wins:**
- No debt or interest payments
- No equity loss
- Shared risk with investors
- Scalable capital per product line
- Incentive to operate efficiently
